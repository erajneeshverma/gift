To my dearest Khushi, 🌸

There are moments in life when words feel entirely inadequate — and yet, I sit here trying to find the right ones, because you deserve to know exactly what you mean to me.

Khushi, I don't know how to explain what it feels like to have you in my life, but I'll try. It's like waking up every morning and knowing that the day is already better, simply because you're in it. You carry a warmth with you that I've never found anywhere else — and somehow, you share it so freely, so genuinely.

I love the way your mind works. The way you see things that others miss. The way you laugh — properly laugh, with your whole self. The way you care about people, about me, about the world around you. These things aren't small. They are everything.

You've made me want to be a better person. Not because you asked me to — but because being around someone like you makes you want to rise. You inspire me without even trying. And I find that quietly extraordinary.

I know that life gets complicated sometimes. That there are hard days and distance and the ordinary weight of things. But I want you to know that through all of it — I am certain. Certain about you. Certain about us.

Today is Girlfriend Day, and while I know every day should feel like a celebration of you, I wanted to use this day to say what I don't say often enough: Khushi, you are my favourite person. You are my calm in the middle of chaos. You are the reason I smile at my phone. You are the person I want to tell things to first.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for trusting me. Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every quiet moment we've shared. Thank you for being exactly who you are — because who you are is someone I am incredibly lucky to know and love.

I built this website for you — because you deserve to have someone create something, just for you. I hope when you read these words, you feel it. Every single bit of it.

Happy Girlfriend Day, Khushi Kumari. You deserve the whole world. 🌸

With all the love I have,

Rajnish Kumar Verma ❤️
